---

Build an assessment for a franchisee application using the extracted information and financial analysis. Include the following steps:

- Verify the completeness and consistency of the extracted information.
- Evaluate the applicant's financial readiness based on the financial analysis.
- Identify any missing or unclear information that requires further review.
- Assess the proposed location's suitability for the franchise.
- Highlight any risks or concerns related to the applicant's financials or projections.
- Recommend approval or rejection, including any conditions or contingencies.
- Document follow-up actions required, such as requesting additional information or clarifications from the applicant.
- Prepare a well-formatted, HTML-based summary of the assessment for communication to relevant stakeholders.
