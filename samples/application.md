# Froyo Foundry franchise application — Historic Dublin, Ohio

## 1. Applicant overview

We, Jordan and Morgan Avery, request consideration for one owner-operated Froyo Foundry location in the Historic Dublin area of the Columbus metropolitan market. We propose a 1,750-square-foot frozen-yogurt shop with self-service machines, a supervised toppings station, dairy-free options, and take-home pints.

Our proposed company is Scioto Spoon Hospitality LLC, with Jordan holding 70% and Morgan 30%. Formation is pending preliminary franchise approval. We seek a single unit initially and are not requesting territorial exclusivity or a multi-unit development commitment.

Jordan will serve as full-time operating owner. Morgan will oversee bookkeeping, scheduling controls, local marketing, and vendor administration for approximately 15 hours weekly. We expect to open June 1, 2027, subject to financing, an executed lease, permitting, construction, and training.
